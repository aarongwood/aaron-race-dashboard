1 · Race packet

How it works

  1. Ask ChatGPT/Codex to assemble the official result, Garmin evidence, athlete report and competitive field into this repository’s race-packet format.
  2. Paste the packet here and press Generate report.
  3. Review the local preview.
  4. Type PUBLISH and press the publish button. Only the packet, its generated page and the report archive are committed.
