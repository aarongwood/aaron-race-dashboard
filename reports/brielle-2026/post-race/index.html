At age 53, Aaron ran 44:11.6 on a relentlessly rolling course and won Male 50–59 by 2 minutes, 38.8 seconds. He finished 19th of 202 overall and 16th of 119 men.

06What made it possible

  1. Accumulated fitness. Months of speed, threshold, hills and endurance created the result.
  2. Permission to race. Aaron arrived fresh and made a clean decision to compete.
  3. Shoe confidence. He chose the trusted ASICS Metaspeed Sky Tokyo.
  4. Terrain intelligence. Pace breathed with elevation while intent stayed constant.
  5. Late-race speed. Cadence and stride were still available after 40 minutes.
  6. Healthy movement. No pain or protective loading was reported.

08Where the next gains live

  • Climbing economy. Preserve quick contact and forward projection as force rises.
  • Moderate-pace economy. Make race-speed organization cheaper at marathon and steady paces.
  • Long-duration durability. Carry mechanics, fueling and tissue resilience beyond two hours.
  • Start calibration. Spend slightly less in the opening minutes on flatter championship courses.
  • Upper-body economy. Loose jaw, shoulders down, elbows back and hands in lane.
