06Race-morning readiness gate

GREEN · Race

Normal gait and stairs, restored sleep, rebounding HRV, ordinary soreness only and a normal warm-up response.

YELLOW · Adjust

Incomplete recovery or heavy legs with normal gait and symptoms no worse than 0–2/10: run controlled and reduce the day.

RED · Stop

Sharp or increasing pain, gait change, illness, dizziness, abnormal fatigue or another severely short night: no hard race.
