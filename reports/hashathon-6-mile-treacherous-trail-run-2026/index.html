06Race-morning readiness gate

GREEN · Race

Normal gait and stairs, ordinary soreness only, stable energy and a warm-up that improves movement: race the supported plan.

YELLOW · Adjust

Heavy legs, incomplete recovery or mild tightness with normal gait: reduce the target, extend the warm-up and reassess.

RED · Stop

Sharp or increasing pain, gait change, illness, dizziness, abnormal fatigue or worsening symptoms: do not race hard.
